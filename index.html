🌿 Beginner's Body Scan

  1. Sit or lie down in a comfortable, relaxed position.
  2. Close your eyes gently and take three slow, deep breaths.
  3. Notice any areas of tension in your body — from head to toe.
  4. With each calm exhale, let the tension soften and dissolve.
  5. When the completion bell sounds, open your eyes slowly.

Meditation is a self-care practice. It is not a substitute for professional mental health support.

5-4-3-2-1 Grounding

~3 min

Notice and name:

  • 👀 5 things you can see
  • 🤲 4 things you can touch
  • 👂 3 things you can hear
  • 👃 2 things you can smell
  • 👅 1 thing you can taste

Take your time with each sense. There's no rush.

💆

Progressive Muscle Relaxation

~5 min
  1. Find a comfortable position — sitting or lying down.
  2. Starting with your feet: tense the muscles tightly for 5 seconds.
  3. Then release completely — notice the relaxation.
  4. Work upward: calves → thighs → stomach → hands → shoulders → face.
  5. End with three slow deep breaths.
🏡

Safe Place Visualization

~4 min
  1. Close your eyes. Take three slow breaths.
  2. Picture a place where you feel completely safe and calm — real or imagined.
  3. Notice the details: What do you see? What sounds are there? How does the air feel?
  4. Spend a few minutes here. You are safe.
  5. When ready, gently bring your awareness back to the room.
🧍

Quick Desk Stretch

~2 min
  • 🔄 Roll your shoulders backwards 5 times
  • ↔️ Gently tilt your head side to side — hold 5s each
  • 🤲 Stretch your arms overhead — take a deep breath
  • 🦵 Stand up and shake out your legs for 30 seconds
  • 😊 Take one deep breath and smile

A gentle reminder that your feelings are valid and you are capable of growth.
